# Behavior-Driven Development

BDD is a way to design and describe software in terms of behavior rather than implementation. Gherkin and `.feature` files are one way to express it, not its definition.

- For behavior-first specification and test naming: Call the Skill tool with "behavior-specification".
- For scenario coverage, writing or revising `.feature` files, and approval: Call the Skill tool with "atdd".
- For bindings and verification of existing or approved scenarios: Call the Skill tool with "hvtdd".
